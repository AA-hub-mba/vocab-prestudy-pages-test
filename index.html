adapt /əˈdæpt/

適應；改編；調整以適合

to change so that something works in a new situation

Inflections: adapts, adapted, adapting

Collocations: adapt to change; adapt to a new environment; adapt a plan; adapt an approach

Word family members

adapt verb · core

適應；調整

verb: change so something works in a new situation
Example: We need to adapt our study plan.

adaptation noun · high

改編；適應

noun: a change made to suit a new situation
Example: The adaptation took several weeks.

adaptable adjective · high

適應力強的

adjective: able to change or be changed for a new situation
Example: She is adaptable in unfamiliar settings.

adaptive adjective · medium

適應性的

adjective: changing in response to conditions
Example: The system uses adaptive practice.

adaptability noun · medium

適應能力

noun: the ability to change for a new situation
Example: Adaptability is useful in a changing workplace.

adaptively adverb · low

以適應性方式

adverb: in a way that changes in response to conditions
Example: The system adjusts adaptively.

Near-synonym notes: adjust — usually a smaller change; modify — change one part of something

approach /əˈprəʊtʃ/

方法；途徑；接近；處理問題

a way of dealing with a problem or situation; to deal with something in a particular way

Inflections: approaches, approached, approaching

Collocations: take an approach; a practical approach; approach a problem; a new approach to learning

Word family members

approach noun / verb · core/high

方法；處理；對待

noun: a way of dealing with a problem
Example: We need a practical approach.

verb: deal with a problem in a particular way
Example: They approached the problem carefully.

approachable adjective · medium

容易接近；平易近人

adjective: friendly and easy to talk to
Example: The teacher was approachable.

approachability noun · low

平易近人；容易接近的特質

noun: the quality of being easy to talk to
Example: Her approachability helped the team.

Near-synonym notes: method — a clearer procedure; strategy — a plan for reaching a goal

benefit /ˈbenɪfɪt/

好處；益處；受益

a helpful result or advantage; to gain help or an advantage

Inflections: benefits, benefited, benefiting

Collocations: the benefits of; benefit from experience; benefit greatly; a significant benefit

Word family members

benefit noun / verb · core/high

好處；益處；受益

noun: a helpful result or advantage
Example: The main benefit is flexibility.

verb: gain help or an advantage
Example: Students benefit from repeated exposure.

beneficial adjective · high

有益的

adjective: producing a helpful result
Example: Regular review is beneficial.

beneficiary noun · medium

受益者

noun: a person or group that receives a benefit
Example: The patient was the main beneficiary.

beneficially adverb · low

有益地

adverb: in a way that produces a helpful result
Example: The changes affected the process beneficially.

beneficiaries plural noun · low

受益者（複數）

plural noun: people or groups that receive a benefit
Example: The beneficiaries received support.

Near-synonym notes: advantage — a favourable condition; profit — usually financial gain
