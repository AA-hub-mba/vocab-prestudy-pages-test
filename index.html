Adapting to change and choosing an approach

2026-11-06 · core · 2026-11-06-core-01

Content version: 2026-11-06-core-01-v2-draft
Status: draft
adapt /əˈdæpt/

適應；調整

to change something so it fits a new situation

Example: We need to adapt our plan when the situation changes.

Collocations: adapt quickly; adapt to change

approach /əˈprəʊtʃ/

方法；處理方式

a way of dealing with a task or problem

Example: This approach gives the team more time to test the idea.

Collocations: practical approach; take an approach

benefit /ˈbenɪfɪt/

好處；益處

an advantage or useful result

Example: One benefit of the new approach is clearer communication.

Collocations: key benefit; benefit from
